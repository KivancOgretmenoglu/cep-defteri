Guardrails

1Rare: at most once per session-minute

One cameo per screen visit, never more than one a minute, and never on two consecutive visits. It stays about 8 seconds, then walks off by itself.

2Read-only screens only

Özet, Bütçe, Yatırım, Raporlar and empty states. Never while a sheet, dialog, keyboard or form is open, and never on add/edit screens.

3Small and non-blocking

One corner slot of about 64 to 80 px (pairs up to 92 px) inside the safe area, above the tab bar. It never overlaps a button, input or amount, and it does not take touches outside its own box.

4Tap to dismiss

Tapping the cameo or its bubble removes it at once. A dismissed cameo does not come back that session. Screen readers get one polite line, nothing focus-stealing.

5Off switch

Settings, Mascot: "Guest cameos" toggle, on by default. Also off when reduced motion is requested (static frame only, no loop) and during onboarding.

6Optional quiet modes

Hidden in exam week (if a student mode is set) and when the main mascot is mid-action, so two mascots never talk at once. Guests never replace the main mascot's quips.

Art spec: sprites are the real 24x24 bodies from characters.ts, drawn at 3 px per art pixel (2 px for pairs) with shape-rendering="crispEdges". Animations are steps() based like mascot.css. Guests are picked from the four non-main characters by scene preference, so the cast changes with the main mascot.
